# Fídíò Brand Guidelines — Visual Identity System

**Brand:** Fídíò  
**Technical Identifier:** `fidio`  
**Product:** Fídíò Studio  
**Tagline:** Imagine. Create. Fídíò.  
**Product Descriptor:** AI-powered creative production  

---

## 1. Brand Essence & Idea

Fídíò transforms human imagination into finished, professional media assets through intelligent orchestration and AI-powered multi-modal generation.

```
Idea ──> AI Understanding ──> Creative Plan ──> Scene Generation ──> Video Composition
```

### Brand Tone & Persona
- **Cinematic:** Deep obsidian surfaces, refined lighting, frame-focused precision.
- **Intelligent:** Clean geometric typography, uncluttered interfaces, high clarity.
- **Expressive:** Signature violet/magenta accent motifs representing creative power.
- **Modern & Credible:** Professional engineering, predictable execution, zero hype.

---

## 2. Logo System & Motifs

The **Fídíò** logotype features custom treatment of the two acute accents above the `í` characters. The dual accent marks serve as a synchronized playhead and frame marker motif, representing timeline progression and synchronized multi-modal AI generation.

### Logo Variants
- `fidio-wordmark-dark.svg`: Primary dark-background wordmark (Warm White typography with Signature Accent playheads).
- `fidio-wordmark-light.svg`: Light-background wordmark (Obsidian typography with Violet playheads).
- `fidio-wordmark-mono-dark.svg`: Single-color warm white wordmark for dark surfaces.
- `fidio-wordmark-mono-light.svg`: Single-color obsidian wordmark for light surfaces.
- `fidio-wordmark-accent.svg`: Full accent gradient wordmark for hero branding.
- `fidio-technical.svg`: Plain ASCII `fidio` wordmark for technical docs, repositories, Docker, and API usages.
- `fidio-symbol.svg`: Standalone geometric mark featuring the dual playhead frame motif inside a aperture circle.
- `fidio-app-icon.svg`: Rounded square app icon with dark obsidian background and glowing signature mark.

## 4. Typography Specification

- **Display Typography:** Inter / Sans-serif (Bold / Semi-bold, geometric line ratios).
- **UI & Body Typography:** Inter (Regular / Medium, optimized for screen legibility).
- **Technical & Code Typography:** JetBrains Mono / Monospace (Used for timestamps, S3 keys, and job IDs).

---

## 5. Incorrect Usage Rules

- ❌ DO NOT remove or misalign the acute accents above the two `í` characters in the primary wordmark.
- ❌ DO NOT use non-ASCII accent characters in code, package names, Docker tags, or URLs (use `fidio`).
- ❌ DO NOT stretch, rotate, distort, or apply drop shadows to the logo files.
- ❌ DO NOT change the signature accent color from Electric Violet/Magenta to generic blue or green.
- ❌ DO NOT place the primary dark wordmark on low-contrast backgrounds.
